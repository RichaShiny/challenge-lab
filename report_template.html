01 / Product framing

From an audience to a habit

Visitors arrive from a creator video. The existing experience offers content and products. The challenger adds a short daily mission, progress feedback, and a completion reward.

A
Control · standard fan hub

Browse content and shop. No missions or rewards.

B
Challenger · seven-day mission

Complete a daily activity, advance through seven steps, and unlock a reward.

Hypothesis: a visible goal increases day-seven returns. Risk: incentives buy activity that costs more than it creates.

04 / Trust before interpretation

A repeatable experiment contract

50/50 stable user assignment. Every assigned user stays in the denominator. D7 counts a return in [168, 192) hours after assignment. No early significance stopping.
